Generated by `tools/audit_price_suffix_skus.py`. **Measurement only - nothing
in the vocabulary, the allocation groups or `Dim_Product` was modified.** The
merge ruling is deferred decision **B6** and belongs to USTore staff.

## The 8 twin families

Each of these has both a priced row and a bare row. Whether the two are the
same physical product is exactly what nobody in the repo can answer.

| Base family | Priced rows | Units (priced) | Bare-row class |
|---|---|---:|---|

| Eco Bag | `Eco Bag @80`, `Eco Bag @ 130` | 351 | F, 270 units |
| ID Case | `ID Case @20` | 18 | F, 455 units |
| Keychain | `Keychain @180`, `Keychain @160` | 709 | N, 0 units |
| Lanyard | `Lanyard @180`, `Lanyard @190` | 7,880 | N, 0 units |
| Long Sticker | `Long Sticker @120` | 217 | N, 0 units |
| New Tiger Plushie Big | `New Tiger Plushie Big @850` | 622 | F, 134 units |
| New Tiger Plushie Small | `New Tiger Plushie Small @750` | 391 | F, 199 units |

**These are two different problems, not one.**

- 4 families have a bare row with **0 units and class N** (`Arch`, `Keychain`, `Lanyard`, `Long Sticker`). Nothing was
  ever recorded against the bare label, so it is almost certainly a vocabulary
  artifact rather than a real product. Merging these would move no units.
- 4 families have a bare row carrying **real sales** (`Eco Bag`, `ID Case`, `New Tiger Plushie Big`, `New Tiger Plushie Small`). Here the bare and
  priced labels were both used, in the same dataset, and merging them *would*
  move units between SKUs and change the FSN split. These are the ones that
  genuinely need a staff ruling.

## Why this is not cosmetic

`Lanyard @180` is **7,201 units** - the largest single SKU in the dataset - and is
Fast-classified. A bare `Lanyard` row also exists, with 0 units and class N.
Anyone asking "how many lanyards did we sell" gets a different answer
depending on which row they read, and the FSN classification depends on the
same choice.

Lanyards are the worst case: **10** distinct lanyard SKUs carry at least one
`Fact_Sales` row (3 price-suffixed, 7 named variants), split 4 F / 6 S.
A further 6 lanyard rows exist with no sales at all.

## What would settle it

A USTore staff member confirming, per family, whether the priced and bare
labels denote one product or two. Until then the rows stay separate, because
merging them is irreversible in the vocabulary and would silently move the
FSN split, the tier counts and every forecast built on them.
